## Product boundary

The workbench implements custody metadata, real byte hashing and verified copying, format policy, rights review, source provenance and draft paperwork. It does not replicate the full Preservica preservation service. Current vendor pricing lists managed storage, integrity checks, access functions and, on higher plans, ongoing format transformation. A customer’s service scope must cover storage, security, conversion, recovery and access experiences before replacement is agreed.
